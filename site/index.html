      What one credit covers

      One credit. One client. One full 35-day round.

      Every credit covers all six parts of the round, postage included. You tell us the strategy, we carry it out.

      Dispute preparation

      Factual and consumer law–based letters prepared from your instructions and your client's documents. Six to nine letters per credit, depending on the profile.

      Metro 2 challenges

      Data challenges formatted to Metro 2 reporting standards, built from the data in your dispute software.

      Print and first-class mail

      We print, stuff, stamp and mail every letter first class, and keep copies on file. Postage is included in the credit.

      CFPB and BBB submissions

      Complaints prepared and filed when you authorize them and the client's documents support them.

      Litigation review

      Files with possible violations go to independent law partners for review, giving your client leverage that makes deletions stick.

      In-system updates

      At the close of each round we update status and results inside your software, so your client always has something new to see.

      Inside a round

      The same rhythm, every 35 days.

      DAYS 1–3Prepare

      We pull the latest report data from your software and build the round's letters and challenges.

      DAYS 3–5Mail

      Letters go out first class. Copies and mail dates are logged.

      DAYS 5–30Bureaus investigate

      Bureaus and furnishers generally have 30 days to respond. We track every deadline.

      DAYS 30–35Update and plan

      Results go into your software and your portal, and the next round is lined up.

      Questions

      Ask before you hand anything off.

      Are you a credit repair company?

      No. We are a back-end fulfillment company for credit repair businesses. We don't sell to consumers or sign contracts with them. Your company stays the credit repair organization, and we do the administrative work for you.

      Do we use your software or ours?

      Yours. We work inside the dispute software you already use, so nothing changes for your clients. You also get our partner portal to see every client, letter and result in one place.

      Will you ever talk to my clients?

      No. For credit repair we never contact your clients. Questions, updates and documents come through you, and you decide what to share.

      What exactly does one credit cover?

      One client for one full 35-day round: letter preparation, Metro 2 challenges, authorized CFPB and BBB submissions, litigation review support, printing, first-class postage and in-system updates.

      Do credits expire?

      No. Buy at the volume rate that fits you and use them as your clients need rounds.

      Do you guarantee results?

      No one can honestly guarantee deletions or score changes. We guarantee the work: every round prepared, mailed and logged on time.

        Questions

        Before you send us your first client.

        Do I need any lending experience?

        No. You refer the client and make the introduction. We handle the application, documents, lender choice, submissions and closing.

        Will you take my client?

        No. The client belongs to you. We work under your brand, you can see every message, and our partner agreement prohibits us from soliciting your clients.

        What kinds of businesses can you fund?

        Most established businesses with steady revenue, plus newer businesses whose owners have strong personal credit. The fundability review tells us which products fit before anything is submitted.

        How long does funding take?

        It depends on the product. Revenue-based financing can fund in days, lines of credit and term loans usually take one to three weeks, and SBA loans take longer.

        I already run credit repair with you. How do the two connect?

        Your graduating credit repair clients are often ready for funding. When a client finishes, we can flag them for a funding review in your portal.

        How much do I earn per deal?

        Half. Every funding fee is split 50/50 between you and us, and your share is paid when the deal funds. You can track every payout in your partner portal.
